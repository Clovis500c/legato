# Changelog

Every version has its notes on the [Releases page](https://github.com/Clovis500c/legato/releases), written from the
`feat`, `fix` and `perf` commits since the previous one (`scripts/notes.mjs`), under Added, Fixed and Performance
improved. The app shows the same notes on its update card. They live there, and not in this file, so there is one
source and it cannot drift.

Two channels (Settings › General › Updates):

- **Stable**: a beta that was tried, promoted by hand. The default.
- **Beta**: every push to `master` that passes the checks, marked *Pre-release*.

The newest Stable is [the latest release](https://github.com/Clovis500c/legato/releases/latest).
